# Three synthetic cases

## Harbor Commercial Services

All figures are fictional USD. FY2025, 12 months, accrual basis.

Historical EBITDA = 450,000 net income + 80,000 interest + 70,000 income-tax expense + 100,000 D&A = **700,000**.

Seller presentation = 700,000 + 220,000 departing-owner compensation + 80,000 legal fees + 50,000 proposed supplier savings = **1,050,000**.

Buyer base case excludes unresolved legal fees and rejects supplier savings. It adds 220,000 for the departing owner, deducts 120,000 replacement compensation, and removes 25,000 of one-time earnings identified by the buyer. Normalized earnings = **775,000**.

Additional recurring operating costs of 30,000 produce operating earnings of **745,000**. Maintenance capex 60,000, growth capex 20,000, incremental working capital 40,000, cash taxes 100,000, and other requirements zero leave **525,000 before financing**. Annual debt service 360,000 leaves **165,000** and illustrative coverage **1.4583×**.

At 5 million EV, normalized multiple = **6.4516×**, compared with **4.7619×** on the seller presentation. The 500,000 seller note is already included in EV.

For −10% revenue change, a 40% contribution margin and 20,000 additional costs: earnings decline = 5,000,000 × −10% × 40% − 20,000 = **−220,000**. Scenario operating earnings = **525,000**; scenario cash = **305,000**; coverage = **0.8472×**.

**Interpretation:** the earnings basis affects the apparent price, and the base case can cover the selected debt service while this downside cannot. This is an assumption-based example, not a deal recommendation.

## Keystone Equipment Services

LTM September 2026; accrual basis; fictional USD. Historical EBITDA = 440,000 + 100,000 + 60,000 + 300,000 = **900,000**. Assumed relocation adjustment +100,000 yields normalized earnings **1,000,000** and a **4.50×** multiple on 4.5 million EV.

Additional opex 50,000 yields operating earnings **950,000**. Maintenance capex 350,000, growth capex 100,000, working capital 80,000, cash taxes 100,000, and other requirements 20,000 leave cash before financing **300,000**. Against 300,000 debt service, coverage is **1.00×** with no residual cash.

At −10% revenue change on 6 million revenue and a 45% contribution margin, earnings decline **270,000** and cash falls to **30,000**, or **0.10×** coverage.

**Interpretation:** a lower EBITDA multiple alone does not establish stronger cash economics.

## Early financial summary

Cash-basis fictional data omit income-tax expense, replacement cost, cash requirements, and price confirmation. The tool displays incomplete adjusted results and no multiple. It identifies the specific omissions rather than substituting zeros.

**Interpretation:** withholding a result can be the correct output. Incomplete inputs are not a low-quality acquisition score.
